NOTES: Every web data point I found is in values. Summary:

1. Not found anywhere (official or vendor): HIVE mass, CELL mass, centre of mass, damper stroke/force/damping, bearing friction, FLOWER pipe positions, Back Skin dimensions. Vendor 'grams' fields are Shopify shipping weights, not assembled masses. Skins are a 5 g placeholder. I gave derived estimates instead: HIVE ≈2.6 kg, CELL ≈1.07 kg, pivot ≈0.48 kg.

2. The damper is a Blum BLUMOTION 970A. The official STEP names it 'am-5865: Blumotion 970A Damper'; AndyMark sells it as am-1762 'Linear Damper, 970A' (nylon). Q12 confirms a TIP counts when the damper contacts the HIVE frame. Checklist 2.7 says the dampers prevent bouncing. Both fit the planned no-bounce stops.

3. Ballast: 5 washers (am-1523, 2.72 g each) plus a wing nut (≈ same mass) per Cell as shipped and in the IFG figure. The count is tuned per field. The official CAD places only the wing nut, no washers.

4. Team Update 01 corrected 'Bottom of HIVE above TILES' to 30.6 in.

5. FLOWER middle-ring height is derived from two controlled values: 21.5 ± 0.25 in top opening and 17.5 ± 0.125 in middle-bottom to top-top. Bottom of Layer B ≈ 4.0 in, top ≈ 5.35 in.

6. CAD numbers came from a read-only Python parse of the official STEP in my scratch folder: pipe OD/ID, damper solid, stepped axle, two Layer B sockets. These are leads for the CAD extraction agent, not a full assembly-transform extraction. The Layer B pipe sockets appear tilted about 2°, which may matter for the cage model.

7. Housekeeping: the official PDFs and the CAD zip were downloaded twice; the second download was byte-identical (sha256) to the first. Text was extracted from the PDFs and HTML for reading (Initial Field Guide, Event Field Guide, Field Acceptance Checklist, Mitigation Guide, CM section 9, Team Updates, Reset Guide, the Q&A pages) and page renders were made to look at figures; those working copies are not kept in the repository, so the line numbers of the form ifg.txt:NNN used above are positions in a local extract and cannot be reproduced (use the section numbers). No repository files were modified.

8. Small discrepancies: the IFG parts list says Churro Lite am-3666, while AndyMark and CAD use am-5867 (10.5 in). Skin width is 11.625 in (IFG) vs 11.75 in (AndyMark). Bottom ring thickness is 0.4 in (CM text) vs 0.43 in (CM figure) vs 0.56 in (AndyMark height, probably includes features).
